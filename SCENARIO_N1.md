# Scenario N1: Video-Driven PDM Scrollytelling

## Core Idea

The landing page is built around a scroll-controlled background video. As the user scrolls, the video advances frame by frame and tells the story of PDM:

> From an artist lost in platform noise to an artist powered by a loyal fanbase.

The video is not just decoration. It is the main narrative layer. Text, UI cards, metrics, fan signals and CTA elements appear on top of the video at specific scroll moments.

## Narrative Arc

PDM shifts the music economy from:

> plays = money

to:

> loyal fans = money

The story should feel premium, cinematic and serious: dark luxury music world, gold light, stage atmosphere, futuristic UI, and a clear emotional movement from isolation to connection.

## Scene 1: The Empty Stage

Visual:

- A dark empty stage or studio.
- One artist silhouette stands alone in a weak spotlight.
- Subtle smoke, cold light, silence, tension.
- The world feels large, expensive and lonely.

Overlay text:

> Streaming made everyone visible.  
> But not everyone connected.

Meaning:

The artist can publish music, but still does not own the relationship with fans.

## Scene 2: The Noise Flood

Visual:

- The camera pulls back.
- Thousands of abstract album covers, waveform lines and track cards appear around the artist.
- The scene becomes beautiful but overwhelming.
- The flood represents AI music, algorithmic content and endless passive uploads.

Overlay text:

> AI floods the feed.  
> Algorithms own the attention.  
> Artists lose the relationship.

Meaning:

The old streaming economy rewards volume and algorithmic visibility, not real loyalty.

## Scene 3: The Fragmented World

Visual:

- Separate floating worlds appear around the artist:
  - music
  - video
  - posts
  - announcements
  - tickets
  - merch
  - chat
  - fan support
- The screens are disconnected and orbit chaotically.

Overlay text:

> Music here.  
> Fans there.  
> Tickets somewhere else.

Then:

> The fanbase is scattered.

Meaning:

Artists currently have to stitch together many platforms just to manage one fan relationship.

## Scene 4: The PDM Pull

Visual:

- A warm golden PDM core appears in the center.
- The fragmented screens begin to move toward it.
- Music, video, posts, chat, tickets and merch assemble into one premium product surface.
- This is the first major wow moment.

Overlay text:

> PDM brings the artist world into one place.

Meaning:

PDM becomes the single operating system for the artist-fan relationship.

## Scene 5: The Fanbase Awakens

Visual:

- Abstract passive track cards fade away.
- Real fan signals appear:
  - avatars
  - chat bubbles
  - subscriber badges
  - hearts
  - ticket access cards
  - merch drops
- Golden lines connect fans to the artist.
- The scene becomes warmer and more alive.

Overlay text:

> Not passive plays.  
> Loyal fans.

Meaning:

The platform is about people, not empty stream counts.

## Scene 6: The New Economy

Visual:

- A golden audio waveform transforms into business signals:
  - subscribers
  - active community
  - ticket demand
  - merch revenue
  - fan retention
- The image should feel like music turning into a living dashboard, not a dry SaaS chart.

Overlay text:

> Loyalty becomes income.  
> Community becomes leverage.  
> Fans become the career engine.

Meaning:

PDM changes what artists optimize for: not passive plays, but fanbase strength.

## Scene 7: The Future Layer

Visual:

- A glowing album capsule, vinyl, tour pass or release object appears.
- Fans gather around it.
- Golden energy fills the object, suggesting future crowdfunding for songs, albums and tours.
- This should feel aspirational, not like a current hard promise.

Overlay text:

> Fund the next song.  
> Launch the next tour.  
> Share the rise.

Meaning:

Future PDM can let fans participate in the success of artists more directly.

## Scene 8: The Final World

Visual:

- The final frame becomes a large premium concert stage.
- The artist is no longer alone.
- A living fanbase network glows in front of the stage.
- PDM is present as a subtle golden identity layer, not a cheap logo splash.

Final text:

> Plays don't build careers.  
> Fans do.

CTA:

- Join as Artist
- Join as Fan

Meaning:

The final emotional state is confidence, scale and belonging.

## Scroll Mapping

Suggested video-to-scroll mapping:

- 0-12%: Empty stage
- 12-28%: AI/content flood
- 28-42%: Fragmented platforms
- 42-58%: PDM pull / assembly moment
- 58-72%: Fanbase awakening
- 72-86%: Loyalty to income transformation
- 86-100%: Final stage and CTA

## Website Overlay Elements

The site should layer HTML/UI elements over the video:

- Short text phrases, not long paragraphs.
- Floating labels for music, video, posts, chat, tickets and merch.
- Dashboard metrics that appear at the new economy moment.
- Fan avatars and subscriber badges.
- Ticket/merch cards.
- Scroll progress indicator, for example `01 / 08`.
- Final premium CTA card.

## Visual Style

The generated video should feel like:

- cinematic dark luxury music world
- black and gold palette
- premium futuristic UI overlays
- volumetric stage lights
- subtle smoke and depth
- realistic but slightly surreal
- high contrast
- expensive Apple / fintech / music documentary mood
- smooth continuous camera movement
- transformation from chaos to organized fanbase network

Avoid:

- cheap neon cyberpunk
- stock concert footage feeling
- random crypto visuals
- unreadable fake logos
- chaotic effects without narrative purpose

## Generation Strategy

It may be easier to generate the video as several strong clips and then stitch them together:

1. Empty artist/stage.
2. AI/content flood.
3. Fragmented platform screens.
4. PDM golden core pulling everything together.
5. Final fanbase network / live stage.

The final landing can use either:

- one continuous video controlled by scroll progress, or
- multiple section videos stitched visually through transitions.

One continuous video is better for the final website, but multiple generated clips may produce higher-quality source material.
