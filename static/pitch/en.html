The problem

An algorithm sits between every fan and every artist.

Fans
  • You follow one artist across five apps: music here, videos there, life everywhere.
  • You pay for a subscription and get little back but fewer ads, while your favourite artists earn fractions of a cent.
  • Bots and scalpers grab the tickets in seconds, then sell them back to you at a markup.
Artists
  • AI is flooding streaming, and no real artist can out-produce a machine.
  • A play is not a fan. Volume will never equal loyalty.
  • Fans pay a subscription, but part of that money goes to artists they never even listen to.
  • You have no real tool to actually work with your fanbase.

Both sides want closer. Nobody built the room.

The insight

Move from pay-per-play to a loyal fanbase you own.

10 loyal fans beat 3,000 anonymous plays

A stream is a transaction. A backer is a relationship — recurring, direct, and impossible for an algorithm to take away.

The solution

Back any artist for $1 a month.

Fans subscribe to a specific artist, not the platform. Listening is free, and $1 unlocks the artist's exclusives — you get closer to them, and become part of their success.

Product

What $1 unlocks — for the fan, and for the artist.

For fans
  • Exclusive drops — private posts, demos and behind-the-scenes.
  • Presale access — first in line for tickets, before the bots.
  • Community & comments — a paid-only chat, and your voice on every track.
  • Participation badges — permanent marks for what you did, not what you paid. Unbuyable, unfarmable.
For artists
  • Direct income — subscriptions land in your account and pay out fast.
  • Your community — a private space to talk to the fans who fund you.
  • Fan analytics — see where your paying fans are, plan tours around them.
Business model

Two revenue lines, so far.

$1/artist/month, an 80/20 split, and a subscription pool distributed by each fan's own listening (user-centric). PDM itself earns from two places:

Transaction commission

20% of every $1 artist subscription — the platform's cut of a recurring, user-centric pool.

Advertising

Shown to listeners who haven't subscribed to an artist yet. Subscribing removes it.
