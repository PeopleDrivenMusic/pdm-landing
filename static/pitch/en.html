The problem · fans

Loving the music has never felt this far from the artist.

  • You follow one artist across five apps: music here, videos there, life everywhere.
  • You pay for a subscription and get little back but fewer ads, while your favourite artists earn fractions of a cent.
  • Bots and scalpers grab the tickets in seconds, then sell them back to you at a markup.

You love the music. You just never get close to the people making it.

The problem · artists

You are fighting an algorithm to reach your own fans.

  • AI is flooding streaming, and no real artist can out-produce a machine.
  • A play is not a fan. Volume will never equal loyalty.
  • Fans pay a subscription, but part of that money goes to artists they never even listen to.
  • You have no real tool to actually work with your fanbase.

One loyal fan beats a thousand blind plays.

The insight

Move from pay-per-play to a loyal fanbase you own.

10 loyal fans beat 3,000 anonymous plays

A stream is a transaction. A backer is a relationship — recurring, direct, and impossible for an algorithm to take away.

Vision · phase 2

Turn songs into an asset class.

Artists can offer a share of a track's future royalties via crowdfunding. Fans buy in with fiat or crypto and become micro-investors — earning not just from plays on PDM, but from Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube too, with PDM acting as the aggregator-licensee.

Fans become owners

Back a track early, share in its upside everywhere it earns.

Artists raise without labels

Fund recording and tours from the people who believe first.

PDM takes a transaction layer

A marketplace fee on every share traded and royalty routed.

Defensibility

Protection from bots and farms.

TrustScore

A two-sided reputation system. Bot streams carry minimal weight, so manipulating payouts becomes economically pointless — and bot farms unprofitable.

Artist grades

Newbie → Active → Creator. Crowdfunding unlocks only after verified, sustained real activity — keeping the exchange clean.

PDM is an incubator, not a label competitor: we prove an artist's market fit, and when they sign, fans can take an "exit" — their shares bought out at a multiple.
